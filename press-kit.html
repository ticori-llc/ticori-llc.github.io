About Ticori

Ticori is a small independent studio making the online games its team always wanted to play with friends: new ways to play together, and always played fair. Its first game is in development.

  • Never pay-to-win. Nothing bought with real money makes you stronger in the game.
  • Never gambling. No loot boxes, no random paid rolls, no casino mechanics.
  • Never paywalled. No content locked behind paywalls, and no game cut up to sell back as DLC. New content ships as updates to the game you already own.
